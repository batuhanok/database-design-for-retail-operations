Problem Definition

Coquette is a chic clothing brand which experiences database design problem. Their current system, while functional, is struggles to handle the growing complexity of their operations. This makes it difficult to track inventory, identify customer trends and optimize workflows.

The biggest issue lies in the lack of data integration. Customer orders are scattered across different platforms, making it challenging to analyze buying patterns and personalize marketing campaigns. Similarly, product information like stock levels and supplier details are often isolated within specific product categories or vendor systems. This makes it difficult for different departments to collaborate on inventory management, leading to inaccurate stock counts and missed purchasing opportunities. This siloed approach also hampers communication and hinders efficient workflows. This disjoint data also hampers employee performance, as they face cumbersome processes and a lack of real-time information.

Coquette has decided to store information about its customers, stores, employees, products, orders, manufacturers and campaigns in a database. The company has wisely chosen to hire us (Batuhan Ok, Mustafa Emre Beyhan, Açelya Sıla Muhçi, and Arda Çiçek) as database designers. The following information describes the situation that the Coquette database must model:

  • Customers are identified by an ID, and have their names, gender, e-mail, phone number, birth date, and addresses must be recorded. Addresses may have more than one value (multi valued).

  • Each of Coquette's stores are identified by an ID, and have a location, region, manager, store target, and store sales.

  • Employees are identified by an ID and a phone number. For each employee, the name, age, title, hire date, employee target, and employee sales must be recorded. In addition, each unique phone number is composed three attributes as area code, number, and extension.

  • Each product is identified by an ID, and has a category, style, color, price, available quantity, and size.

  • Each order received by Coquette is identified by an order number, and has a order date, order quantity, order price, order status, shipping address, and payment method.

  • Each manufacturer of Coquette stores is identified by an ID and has a name and contact person. Additionally, each contact person has an e-mail and phone number.

  • Each campaign is identified by ID and has a name, start date, end date, and available amount.

  • Each customer may use several campaigns, and a given campaign may be used by several customers. Some campaigns may not have customers and some customers may not use the campaigns.

  • Each customer may place several orders, and a given order must be placed by a customer. Every customer must has a order, and vice versa.

  • Each employee must works for a store, and a given store may be employs several employee. Every employee must has a store, and vice versa. Additionally, each manager must manages a store, and a given store managed by a manager. Every store must be managed by a employee(manager).

  • Each supervisor (a employee) may supervise several employees, and each employee must be supervised by a supervisor. Every supervisor must supervises an employee.

  • Each order must belong to a store, and a store may include several orders. Every order must has a store.

  • Each order must include a product, and a product may include several orders. Every order must has a product.

  • Each manufacturer may manufacture several products, and a product must be manufactured by a manufacturer. Every product must has a manufacturer, and vice versa.
